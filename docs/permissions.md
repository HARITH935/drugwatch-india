# Permissions

Republishing permissions required by source owners' copyright policies (ADR 0003, `docs/data-sources.md` §4).
**CDSCO and Jan Aushadhi data must not appear on the live site until permission is recorded here.**

## Draft 1 — CDSCO

**To:** dci@nic.in
**Subject:** Request for permission to reproduce NSQ drug alert data with attribution (non-commercial public-interest project)

> Dear Sir/Madam,
>
> I am [Your name], [student / developer at …]. I am building **DrugWatch India**, a free, non-commercial
> website that helps the public check whether a specific medicine batch appears in CDSCO's published
> Not of Standard Quality (NSQ) and spurious drug alerts.
>
> As your website's copyright policy requires permission before reproduction, I request permission to:
>
> 1. **Reproduce the fields of the NSQ / spurious alerts** published on cdsco.gov.in and
>    cdscoonline.gov.in (product name, batch number, manufacturing and expiry dates, manufacturer,
>    test result, reporting laboratory/state and alert month), accurately and without alteration.
> 2. **Link to the original alert pages and documents** on your websites, opening them in a new window
>    and never inside a frame.
>
> Every record on the site would show its batch number, the alert month, a link to the CDSCO source and
> the credit *"Source: Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO)"*. Every page would carry the note
> *"Not medical advice. Data from CDSCO public alerts."* The site would state that each finding applies only to
> the specific batch tested, not to the product or manufacturer as a whole, and would use neutral wording.
>
> The data would be collected from your public pages about once a month, at a low request rate. If you prefer
> another way to obtain it, such as a bulk download, I would be glad to use that instead.
>
> The project's source code is public at https://github.com/HARITH935/drugwatch-india. I would be happy to
> make any changes you require.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> [Your name]
> [Phone / email]

## Draft 2 — PMBI (Jan Aushadhi)

**To:** it@janaushadhi.gov.in
**Subject:** Request for permission to reproduce the Jan Aushadhi product and MRP list with attribution (non-commercial project)

> Dear Sir/Madam,
>
> I am [Your name], [student / developer at …]. I am building **DrugWatch India**, a free, non-commercial
> website that helps the public understand medicine quality and prices in India.
>
> One of its features shows, for a given medicine, the **Jan Aushadhi MRP** next to the government ceiling
> price, so that people can see the savings available at Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Kendras.
>
> As your website's copyright and hyperlinking policies require prior permission, I request permission to:
>
> 1. **Reproduce the Product & MRP List** published on janaushadhi.gov.in (drug code, generic name,
>    unit size, MRP and group), accurately and without alteration.
> 2. **Link to janaushadhi.gov.in** pages, opening them in a new window and never inside a frame.
>
> Every price shown would carry the credit *"Source: Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBI)"*,
> the date it was collected and a link to your website, with a note that prices are for information only.
>
> The list would be collected from your public website about once a month, at a low request rate. If you
> prefer another way to obtain it, such as a downloadable file, I would be glad to use that instead.
>
> The project's source code is public at https://github.com/HARITH935/drugwatch-india. I would be happy to
> make any changes you require.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> [Your name]
> [Phone / email]
